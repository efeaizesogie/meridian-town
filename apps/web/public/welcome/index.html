Play, earn, own.

What you make in Meridian lands in your own NEAR account, and 12 recovery words make it yours.

  • Sell your harvest

    Shops pay most for what they lack. Read the board and sell where it pays.

  • Run deliveries

    Short shops post contracts with the coin held up front. Deliver and get paid.

  • Own a shop

    Win a failing shop at auction and take half its daily profit as dividends.

  • Share the Festival

    When the town hits its weekly target, new coin goes to everyone who traded.

Yours to keep, sell or move

Coins are MERI tokens and shops are NFT deeds, held under your name. Your recovery words open the account in any NEAR wallet, and every coin and deed in it is yours.

This is the opening season on NEAR testnet, where coins are for practice. When Meridian moves to mainnet, MERI and deeds become tradable tokens. Earnings depend on how the town trades, so we make no promises about amounts.

Built on NEAR.

Every coin, shop and vote is on chain, and you can check each one.

Rhea
